Privacy Policy — work log.

This policy explains what work log. does and doesn't do with your data.

Last updated: [MONTH DAY, YEAR]

Summary

work log. is built by Impulsecheck LLC ("we," "us"). The app is designed so your shift, pay, and attendance data stays on your device. We do not operate a server that collects, stores, or has access to the entries you log.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — job profiles, categories, shift entries, pay rates, overtime rules, deductions, and PTO balances — is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to Impulsecheck LLC, and we have no ability to view it.

If you use the app's backup or export feature, the resulting file is written to a location on your device (or a cloud location you choose, such as Google Drive) that you control. We never receive a copy.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — used to remind you to log a shift. Handled entirely on-device.
  • Biometric / app lock — authentication is handled by your device's operating system. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, or PIN.
  • File storage access — used only when you choose to create a backup or import one. The app doesn't read files you haven't selected.

Advertising (free tier)

The free tier of work log. displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours: policies.google.com/privacy. Upgrading to Premium removes ads and this data collection along with it.

Payments

Premium subscriptions are purchased and processed through Google Play Billing. Impulsecheck LLC does not receive or store your payment card details.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your shift or pay data with any third party, because we don't have access to it in the first place.

Your control over your data

Because your data lives on your device, deleting an entry or category removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, except for any backup files you've separately exported and saved elsewhere.

Children's privacy

work log. is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to contact@impulsecheck.com.
